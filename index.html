⚠ Known Limitations — Transparent Engineering
🌍GPS/Lat-Lon: True geographic coordinates require a DJI .SRT telemetry file alongside the video. Without it, the coordinate system is metric-consistent but not anchored to real-world lat/lon.
🏘️Semantic accuracy: HSV-based segmentation works well for high-altitude nadir imagery. For oblique neighbourhood footage (30–60m altitude), buildings, roads and trees will be more distinctly separated and classified more accurately.
🧊Geometric fusion: Photometric MVS was used (geometric consistency pass produced empty depth maps — a known COLMAP Windows/CUDA driver incompatibility). The 13.3M photometric points are verified and structurally accurate.
⏱Processing time: Full pipeline on RTX 3050 takes ~2.5–3 hours for a 70-second video. Real-time processing is not currently supported.
